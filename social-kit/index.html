02 · Grid & sizes

Three canvases, one structure

Every canvas stacks the same four zones: receipt strip, message, legal line (promotions only), settlement footer. Landscape puts the message in two columns; portrait and story stack it.

SizeSide marginTop / bottomGap unitSafe-zone rule
L · 1200×6305640 / 3622Keep text in the left 60%. FB crops nothing, but IG shows it at 1.91:1 small.
P · 1080×13508064 / 5632IG grid previews the centre 1080×1080: keep the hook between y 135–1215.
S · 1080×192088220 / 30036Top 220 px and bottom 300 px sit under Story UI. Nothing readable there.

Spacing uses one gap unit per size (22 / 32 / 36 px). Elements inside the message zone are separated by 1 gap; the strip's dashed rule sits 0.6 gap below its labels; checklists use 0.5 gap.

03 · Brand elements

Four things on every post

Receipt strip

Top of every canvas. Left: content code in mono (TIP · 014, GUIDE · 007, OFFER · 012). Right: a status chip with a dot. Closed by a dashed rule, like a till receipt. Number posts sequentially per pillar.

Status chip

Pill with a coloured dot: teal on Paper, gold on Vault, Vault on Gold. Two or three words. It tells the scroller what kind of post this is before they read it.

Karma Ring

The gold ring (what goes around comes around), always cropped off the bottom-right corner at 38% of its size. White on Gold posts, 45% opacity on Paper. Dropped on carousel steps and announcements so content can breathe.

Settlement footer

Wordmark left, URL or carousel dots right. Same position on every size. The wordmark here is a type-set placeholder: replace .kc-mark with your logo SVG at the same height (34 / 48 / 52 px).

Signature moves

  • The gold highlight. One phrase per headline, the promise (“under 1%”, “Get approved”). Marker underline on Paper, gold text on Vault.
  • The Declined strike. Coral line through “Declined”, followed by the positive word. Use on promotions and covers, at most once a month.
  • Numbers as heroes. Tip and step numbers set in Display size. A merchant should be able to say “tip 14” in a DM.

05 · Templates

Eight layouts × three sizes

Educational tip

TipsPaper

Weekly how-to nuggets: chargebacks, descriptors, PCI, reserves, documentation.

Text hierarchy

  1. Receipt strip: TIP · ### + “Merchant tip” chip
  2. Tip number in Display size, Settle Teal
  3. Eyebrow (topic, mono caps)
  4. H1 headline with one gold highlight
  5. Body: one or two sentences
  6. Footer: “Save this for later ↓”

Copy limits

Headline ≤ 55 characters · body ≤ 140 · one highlight per headline

Image placement

No photo. The number is the visual.

Carousel cover

TutorialsVault

Opens every multi-slide tutorial or checklist. Always followed by step slides and a closing CTA.

Text hierarchy

  1. Strip: GUIDE · ### + “N steps” chip
  2. Content bottom-aligned (thumb-stopping weight sits low)
  3. Eyebrow → Display headline (gold highlight on the promise)
  4. One-line body, then “Swipe to start →”
  5. Progress dots in footer: first dot is the long gold pill

Copy limits

Headline ≤ 45 characters · 3–8 steps · 1080×1350 for IG carousels

Image placement

No photo. Optional: swap the ring for a product screenshot cropped at the same corner.

Carousel step

TutorialsPaper

One idea per slide. Repeat the layout; only the number, headline, body and checklist change.

Text hierarchy

  1. Strip chip: “Step N of N”
  2. Big step number (Display, Vault) + mono “STEP” label
  3. H1 action headline (starts with a verb)
  4. Body: why it matters
  5. Checklist: up to 4 ✓ items
  6. Dots: current step highlighted in teal

Copy limits

Headline ≤ 40 characters · ≤ 4 checklist items, ≤ 32 characters each · no Karma Ring (keeps slides calm)

Image placement

None. Use a screenshot only when the step is about a form or dashboard.

Carousel closing CTA

TutorialsVault

Last slide of every carousel. The only carousel slide with a button.

Text hierarchy

  1. Strip chip: “Next step”
  2. Eyebrow → Display headline
  3. Body with the response-time promise
  4. Gold pill CTA with URL
  5. Dots: last dot active

Copy limits

One CTA only · button text ≤ 32 characters

Image placement

None.

Quote

QuotesGold

Brand point of view, founder notes, industry insight. Max one in every six posts so gold stays special.

Text hierarchy

  1. Strip: NOTE · ### + chip (“From our team”)
  2. Oversized white quote mark
  3. H1 quote text, Vault
  4. Attribution with rule, mono caps
  5. White Karma Ring

Copy limits

Quote ≤ 90 characters · attribute only real people who approved the wording, otherwise “The Karma Card team”

Image placement

No photo. Optional 1:1 headshot can replace the quote mark.

Announcement

Promotions / newsVault

New industry pages, new payment methods, office hours, events, partner news.

Text hierarchy

  1. Strip chip: status (“Now onboarding”, “New”, “Event”)
  2. Eyebrow → Display headline
  3. Body: what it means for the merchant
  4. Meta row: up to 3 gold facts
  5. Photo panel (right on L, below on P/S)

Copy limits

Headline ≤ 50 characters · 3 meta facts, ≤ 20 characters each

Image placement

Photo slot: L 5:6 right half · P full width, flexible height · S 560 px tall. Duotone Vault overlay 20–35%.

Testimonial

Social proofPaper

Merchant reviews and case results, only with written permission from the merchant.

Text hierarchy

  1. Strip: REVIEW · ### + “Merchant story” chip
  2. Five gold stars only if the review gave five stars
  3. H2 quote in quotation marks
  4. Round 1:1 photo + name + industry/city

Copy limits

Quote ≤ 120 characters · first name + last initial · never invent or edit a review's meaning

Image placement

Round 1:1 headshot or storefront. Real customer photos only, no stock faces.

Promotion

PromotionsVault

Fee offers, switch-over campaigns, seasonal pushes.

Text hierarchy

  1. Strip chip: urgency (“Limited time”, “This month”)
  2. Eyebrow → Display headline (the Declined strike is the signature move)
  3. Gold CTA pill
  4. Offer coupon: dashed gold border, big gold figure
  5. Legal line above footer (required)

Copy limits

Offer figure ≤ 10 characters · legal line always present · end dates stated in caption

Image placement

No photo. The coupon is the visual.

06 · Imagery

Photo direction

Use

  • Real merchants at work: counters, warehouses, studios, clinics.
  • Card terminals and checkout screens in context.
  • Duotone treatment: photo at 100%, Vault multiply layer at 20–35%.
  • Faces looking toward the text side of the canvas.

Avoid

  • Handshakes, piles of cash, stock “business people”.
  • Product imagery for restricted categories (firearms, CBD, adult, gambling). Meta may limit or reject the post.
  • Text baked into photos. All text stays live in the template.
  • Logos of card networks unless you follow their brand rules.

07 · Compliance

Rules for a high-risk processor

  • No guaranteed approvals. Say “open to all industries” or “apply and hear back in 24–48 hours”, never “guaranteed approval”.
  • Legal line on every offer. “Approval subject to underwriting. Rates and terms vary by industry and processing history.” Add end dates in the caption.
  • Check every claim against the website. “No setup fees” and approval-rate figures must match current terms before posting.
  • Testimonials need written permission and must be the merchant's own words. The sample tag on the template is there to stop a placeholder going live.
  • Boosted posts about financial services fall under Meta's special ad categories in some regions, which limits targeting. Plan audiences with that in mind.

08 · Production

Pre-post checklist

  1. Right layout for the pillar, right colour mode, and it doesn't repeat the previous post's mode.
  2. Receipt code updated and sequential (TIP · 015 follows 014).
  3. One highlight in the headline. Copy within the character limits.
  4. Nothing readable in Story safe zones (top 220 px, bottom 300 px).
  5. Legal line present on promotions; sample tag removed from real testimonials.
  6. Export PNG at native size: 1200×630, 1080×1350, 1080×1920.
  7. Caption: hook in the first 125 characters, a CTA, the URL, 3–5 hashtags at most.

Files: exports/png/ holds the rendered samples, exports/express/ one Adobe Express import file per size, and src/templates.mjs the sample copy you edit to make new posts. Run npm run build to regenerate everything.
